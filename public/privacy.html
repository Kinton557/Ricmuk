Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you order, request a quote, send a message or create an account we collect your name, phone number, email (optional), delivery address (if you choose delivery) and what you ordered. If you create an account we also store a securely hashed password, never the password itself.

How we use it

We use your details only to process and deliver your order, reply to your enquiries, keep order records and run the shop. We do not sell your information.

Visitor statistics

We count page visits using a random anonymous visitor ID stored in your browser. It does not identify you and is not shared with advertisers.

Cookies and storage

We use a sign-in cookie if you log in, and your browser’s local storage to remember your cart, your theme (light or dark) and your anonymous visitor ID.

Sharing

Your details are seen only by Ricmuk staff who need them to serve you. If you choose to message us on WhatsApp, that conversation is handled by WhatsApp under its own policy.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your account and order details by contacting the shop. Order records may be kept for accounting purposes.

Contact

Questions? See our Contact page.
