Who we are

Everything you need, in one place

Ricmuk Enterprises is a one stop hardware shop in Rimuka Light Industry, Kadoma. Whether you are roofing a new house, fencing a yard, tiling a bathroom or fitting out a shop, we stock what you need and we help you pick the right thing the first time.

We manufacture IBR and Q-tile roofing sheets, cut to your length in 0.3mm and 0.4mm, and we supply steel, cement, timber, doors, plumbing, electrical, tiles, pumps and water tanks for homes, businesses and industry.
